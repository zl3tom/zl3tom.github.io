ARISS Contact with Australian Students24 August 2020 · Chris Cassidy · KF5KDR＋

On 24 August 2020, students taking part in the i-Educate Conference in Queensland, Australia, made a live amateur radio contact with NASA astronaut Chris Cassidy aboard the International Space Station. The students asked about scientific research, astronaut training, teamwork, daily life in orbit and what it is like to work outside the station.

The contact was supported by amateur radio ground station AB1OC in New Hampshire, USA, operated by Fred Kemmerer. The school session gave students an opportunity to speak directly with a crew member in orbit and learn how science and communication work aboard the ISS.

Questions covered research in space, becoming an astronaut, resolving disagreements as a crew, future space tourism, the perception of time in orbit, hygiene and showering, and experiences during spacewalks.

After the scheduled school contact, the recording continues with an amateur radio callback session. Radio amateurs from different locations were invited to call in, including stations in Australia and New Zealand. Thomas Bernard, ZL3TOM, is among the stations heard during this callback portion.
