Install the Radio Companion

Keep ZL3TOM guides and radio references handy as an installable web app on your phone, tablet or PC.

The Radio Companion can work offline after its site cache has been downloaded.

Android

Open ZL3TOM in Chrome. Use Install app or Add to Home screen, then launch it from your home screen.

iPhone & iPad

Open ZL3TOM in Safari, choose Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Windows / PC

Open ZL3TOM in a supported Chromium browser such as Chrome or Edge and choose the browser's install-app option.

What works offline?

The installed app caches ZL3TOM pages and local resources. Live external services such as QRZ, maps and other websites still need internet access. The NZ Repeater Finder keeps its last successfully downloaded repeater list locally for offline searching.
