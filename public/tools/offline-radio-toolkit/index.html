RST reports

R Readability: 1–5
S Signal strength: 1–9
T Tone for CW: 1–9.

A voice report such as 59 means perfectly readable with a very strong signal.

Calling CQ

“CQ CQ CQ, this is [callsign], [callsign], [callsign], calling CQ and listening.”

Pause and listen before calling again.

Repeater checklist

Check output frequency, input/offset, CTCSS tone if required, and listen before transmitting. Give your callsign clearly and leave sensible gaps between overs.
