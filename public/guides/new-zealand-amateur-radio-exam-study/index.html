ZL3TOM beginner guide

New Zealand Amateur Radio Study Guides & Practice Exams

Prepare for the New Zealand amateur radio exam with NZART study books, question banks, local clubs, Ham Cram training and an embedded practice-exam generator.

← All guides
01

Start with your local radio club

Amateur radio offers local and worldwide contacts, electronics experiments, satellites and digital communication. You can learn independently or ask a club for help. Start with the NZART club directory ↗ and ask about tuition, examination arrangements and obtaining your certificate and callsign.

Some clubs run intensive Ham Cram weekends combining learning and an examination. Check the Ham Cram calendar ↗ and ask the organiser about preparation, bookings and fees.

04

Use practice to build understanding

  1. Read a section of the study book and work through the relevant questions.
  2. Practise a topic you find difficult, then try a mixed mock exam.
  3. Review mistakes with the study book or a club instructor.
  4. When ready, ask your local club to arrange the official examination.

A practice result is for revision only. It does not issue a GAOC or callsign. Your club or examiner can explain the official process and any support you need.
