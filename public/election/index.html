Notable outcomes

What made this election unusual?

A few of the stand-out details from the preliminary figures and election-night reporting.

  • 131 seats projected. Eleven overhang seats would make this the largest Parliament yet, above the previous record of 123.
  • 0.2 percentage points apart. National and Labour were separated by just 0.2 points on election night, with 99.9% of votes counted.
  • More than 40% for minor parties. National and Labour together fell below 60% for the first time in the MMP era.
  • A first for a major party. National's electorate wins were projected to exceed its party-vote entitlement, leaving it without list seats.
  • Opportunity enters Parliament. The party was projected at 6%, with Qiulae Wong set to become the first Asian leader of a party in Parliament.
  • Strong Green result. The Greens' 13.3% was just below the 1996 NZ First record for a third party, 13.35%.

Source basis: preliminary election-night figures and reporting dated 8 November 2026. These are not final certified results; projections may change after special votes.
