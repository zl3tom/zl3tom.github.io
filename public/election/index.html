Notable outcomes

What made this election unusual?

A few of the stand-out details from the preliminary figures.

  • 131 seats in Parliament. The preliminary allocation is 71 electorate seats and 60 list seats.
  • Labour narrowly led the party vote. Labour received 29.36%, just ahead of National on 29.21% — a margin of 0.15 percentage points.
  • Opportunity enters Parliament. The Opportunity Party received 5.96% of the party vote and is allocated seven seats.
  • National wins 42 electorates. Its preliminary allocation has no list seats, while Labour has 21 electorate and 15 list seats.
  • Special votes matter. About 399,365 special votes are estimated, including 96,947 overseas and dictation votes.
  • Strong Green result. The Greens' 13.3% was just below the 1996 NZ First record for a third party, 13.35%.

Source: Electoral Commission preliminary-results release, 7 November 2026. Figures are preliminary and may change after special votes.
