THE MEETING EXPERIENCE

A varied, lively
evening of learning.

Each meeting is structured, but no two are exactly the same. Members take on different roles and help keep the evening moving.

01

Prepared speeches

Members present planned speeches to practise structure, delivery and connecting with an audience.

02

Table Topics®

Short, impromptu responses help participants practise organising thoughts and speaking without preparation.

03

Evaluations

Speakers receive encouraging, specific feedback that recognises strengths and suggests ways to improve.

04

Meeting roles

Members practise listening, timekeeping, introductions, facilitation and other leadership skills.
