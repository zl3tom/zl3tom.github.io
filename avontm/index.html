A PLACE TO FIND YOUR VOICE

You don't need to be a great speaker to get started.

You just need a place to practise — and people who want to see you do well.

Maybe you want to feel calmer when introducing yourself, deliver a presentation at work, contribute confidently in meetings, or simply stop avoiding the spotlight. At Avon Toastmasters, you learn by doing, with friendly people beside you and useful feedback to help you improve.

SKILLS FOR REAL LIFE

More than just
public speaking.

Build practical skills you can take into your workplace, classroom, community and everyday conversations.

01 / SPEAK

Public speaking

Shape your ideas, organise a message and deliver speeches with greater clarity and confidence.

02 / THINK

Think on your feet

Try Table Topics and practise giving thoughtful answers when you haven't had time to prepare.

03 / CONNECT

Communication

Develop listening, body language, vocal variety and the ability to connect with an audience.

04 / LEAD

Leadership

Take meeting roles, support fellow members and build teamwork through hands-on experience.

A MEETING WITH PURPOSE

Learn by taking
part.

Toastmasters meetings are practical, varied and welcoming. You can visit as a guest and see the format for yourself — no preparation needed.

01

PREPARED SPEECHES

Ideas worth sharing

Members deliver prepared speeches to practise structure, storytelling, delivery and presenting with purpose.

02

TABLE TOPICS

Speak in the moment

Short impromptu talks give members a fun way to think quickly and express an idea clearly.

03

HELPFUL EVALUATIONS

Feedback that moves you forward

Speakers receive encouraging, practical feedback that celebrates strengths and suggests what to try next.
